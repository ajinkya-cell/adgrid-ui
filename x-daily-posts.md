# void/ui · daily X posts

One component a day, every day, until it blows up. 111 components, so 111 days.

## How to post

- **Free X means 280 characters.** Every post here is checked against X's own count: emoji and symbols like ⌘ ✓ ⋯ count as 2, and any link counts as 23. The count is next to each post.
- **Always attach a video.** A 5–15s screen recording of the component is the real post. The **Record** line says what to capture.
- **Put the link in the first reply, not the post.** X shows posts with outside links to fewer people. Post, then reply to yourself with the **Reply** text.
- **Post at the same time every day**, then spend 15 minutes replying to other people building in public. That's where the first followers come from.
- The days run strongest first, so the first two weeks show your most visual work.
- When you ship something new, make it the next day's post: it's the freshest thing you have.
